In today's busy and chaotic world, people are unaware of the various environmental/lifestyle issues in every day life that are causing serious danger to the eco system. Also, there is a lack of common platform for bringing everyone together to act upon the issues and reduce the impact of these problems

Our Application is a one stop solution for all the problems revolving around the Sustainable Living in today's world. The main goal of our application is to create awareness on the various issues in our enviroment and current lifestyle. It also showcases the feasible solutions which helps in experiencing a healthy and happy life while adopting the principles of sustainable living.
